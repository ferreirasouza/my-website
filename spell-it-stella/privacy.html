About Information Technology
Web app notice · November 23, 2026

Playing and saving

This web game does not ask for an account, a child's name, an email address, or a login. It stores your game, score, sound preference, hearts and recovery timing in this browser. These saves are separate from the Android app. They are not uploaded by the game or synchronized between devices.

Your browser can download game pictures, animations, fonts and recorded voices for offline play. The game shows when that download is complete. Your browser may remove stored data when space is low. Clearing this site's browser data removes saved progress and downloaded files; uninstalling an icon may not clear browser data.

Network use

When online, the browser requests game files and updates from the website's hosting service. The hosting service receives the connection information needed to serve those requests, such as an IP address and browser information. The game does not add analytics, remote accounts, or a microphone recording feature. Game speech plays from packaged audio files.

Advertising in this release

Advertising is disabled in this web release. No advertising provider is active, no advertising script is loaded, and the game sends no ad requests. You can play and recover hearts without watching an advertisement.

The game is prepared for a future optional rewarded format from a supplier suitable for young children. No supplier has been approved for this release. Before activation, we must confirm that the supplier permits rewarded advertising in this child-directed web game and installed PWA, with appropriate contextual advertising and child protections. This notice will identify the actual supplier and its data practices before any advertising is enabled.

If a future reviewed release offers an ad, it will clearly offer one heart for a valid completed reward. Closing, skipping, starting, clicking, or simply reaching the end of an ordinary video will not earn a heart. The free heart recovery option will remain available. Advertising scripts, responses and media are never part of the game's offline download.

For parents and guardians

For questions about Spell It Stella, contact business@aboutinf.com. You can also visit About Information Technology and the existing privacy page.
