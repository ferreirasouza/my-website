Spell It Stella
Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Spell It Stella is an English spelling game for young children, operated by About Information Technology. This policy explains the Android app to parents and guardians. Contact: business@aboutinf.com.

Playing and learning

The core game works offline, without accounts, chat, purchases, or content uploads. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access. We operate no game-server, cloud-save, or separate analytics backend.

Voices are prerecorded files included in the app; some were generated with OpenAI tools during development. Gameplay does not contact OpenAI, record children, or send their information to OpenAI.

Information stored on your device

The app privately stores words and placed letters, scores, learning progress, remaining time, pause state, sound preferences, heart balance, recharge times, and records preventing duplicate rewards. These support fair resumption after interruptions. Saves stay on your device; app backup and transfer are disabled. Local diagnostic logs are not uploaded to us.

Optional rewarded advertisements

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads for one extra heart. Watching is voluntary; declining or closing an ad does not remove existing hearts or progress. Rewards require confirmation from the ad provider. Free heart recharge remains available.

Preparing or loading an ad can contact Google before you press Watch.

Information processed by Google AdMob

Google's ad SDK can process IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions, performance or crash diagnostics, and technical device or app identifiers permitted by its configuration. Google uses this information for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. SDK transmissions are encrypted.

We configure child-directed treatment, non-personalized advertising, and G-rated ad content. These protections limit data use but do not eliminate technical data collection. We do not receive children's advertising profiles. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for Google's practices.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local saves remain until replaced during play, cleared in Android's app-storage settings, or removed by uninstalling. Clearing app data removes progress and preferences. We cannot retrieve or erase local saves remotely. Clearing local data does not delete Google's records; Google's retention rules and privacy controls apply to those records.

If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We retain support correspondence as necessary to respond and meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending children's names, photographs, recordings, or unnecessary personal information.

Depending on applicable local law, parents and guardians may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information, and to complain to their privacy regulator. Contact us at the email above for questions or requests.

Changes to this policy

We will revise this page and its date when practices change. Linked third-party websites have their own policies.
